Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive the details you type in: your name, business name, email, phone number (if you give it) and your message. When we make a sample commercial for your business, we use public information about it, such as its name, logo, services and contact details from its website or business listings.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to you, to make and deliver commercials, and to run our business. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it except with the services we rely on to do this work, such as email, file storage and payment processing.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Emails from us

If we email you and you'd rather not hear from us, reply and tell us, or use the unsubscribe link in the email, and we'll stop.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Send us a note through the contact form.
